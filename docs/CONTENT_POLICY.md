# Content policy

Naminal Hub tells original stories supported by current animal research.
Distinguish wild and captive behavior, label uncertainty, reject unsupported
superlatives and precise measurements, and avoid unsafe wildlife advice or
fictional rescues. Competitor metadata informs category research only; never
copy titles, scripts, footage, voice or branding. Media and narration must
depict the species and behavior stated. The operator inspects every exact
revision before local export; export never means publication.
